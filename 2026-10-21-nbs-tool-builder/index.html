Build our NbS tool.

The Interactive Map is the part of the NbS Map Platform most people will use, and the part we build ourselves. Starting from SCeNe's NbS tool, each table builds its own version for the Philippines, for one person, step by step: what do we keep, what don't we need, and what do we wish it had?

For participants

Everything happens on this website. Your table builds on one laptop: one person clicks, and everyone decides together. You can follow along on your phone too.

  1. Choose your person

    Open Build our tool and pick who your table builds for.

    • Choose one of the people: , and read their story together. Their stories are also in the card guide.
    • None of them fits? Create your own: choose Someone else and describe your person in a few words, who they are and what they need.
    • At every card, ask: would our person use this?

  2. Watch the step

    In each round, BNHR shows one step of SCeNe's tool on the big screen. If you like, try it yourself in SCeNe's interactive map, or look at the step's cards in the card guide.

  3. Choose for each card

    On your board, choose one for each card:

    • Keep: our person needs this. It uses one of your keeps.
    • Maybe: we're not sure. It doesn't count either way.
    • We don't need it: our person wouldn't use it. Your board asks why: a few words are enough.
    • You don't have to choose for every card. If your table can't agree or runs out of time, leave the card without a choice and move on.
    • Add a note to any card if you like, for example: keep it, but with official flood maps.
    • Changed your mind? Click the same choice again to clear it.

  4. Use your keeps wisely

    • Your table can keep of the cards, counted across all the steps together, not per step.
    • Keep more in the steps that matter most to your person, and save some for the later steps.
    • Your board shows how many keeps are left. None left? Change a Keep to Maybe to free one up.

  5. Add your wishes

    Is something missing from the cards? Add a wish at the bottom of the step, with:

    • what is missing
    • what information it would need
    • who has that information.

    Wishes don't use up your keeps, and they can get card dots.

  6. Give your priority dots

    Keeps say what your person needs. Priority dots say how much each one matters to them.

    • card dots go on your kept cards and wishes. Other cards can't get them.
    • The more dots a card gets, the more important it is to your person. Put several on one card if it matters a lot. A kept card with no dots is still kept.
    • data dots are separate. They go on the data the tool needs most, in the Data we need tab.
    • On the big screen, the cards and wishes with the most dots from all the tables come first.

  7. Tell us about the data

    In the Data we need tab, for each kind of data:

    • Do we need it?
    • Why?
    • Can your organisation share it?
    • If not, where is it, or who has it?

    Add any data that's missing from the list, including your own NbS projects.

  8. Hand it in

    When BNHR asks, your host clicks Download our board and sends the file to BNHR. All the tables' boards then appear together on the big screen.

Joining online? Do the same on your own computer: open Build our tool, and your online host will guide you through the call.

For table hosts

You help your table through the rounds, keep time and make sure everyone is heard. You don't need to know the tool: the cards explain themselves.

Before the session

  1. Open Build our tool on your table's laptop and keep the tab open all session. Once it's open, the board keeps working without internet and is saved in the laptop's browser.
  2. Type your table's name (for example, Table 2) and choose the session.
  3. Read the people's stories in the card guide, and skim the cards.
  4. Check with BNHR how to send your board file: the shared folder or the group chat.

During the rounds

  1. Help the table choose its person first, and read the story aloud. If none of them fits, the table can create its own: choose Someone else and describe them.
  2. Keep to time: about 12 minutes a round, and about 6 of those for choosing. Round 2 (What's there) has cards: share them out, one person reading each card aloud.
  3. Ask at every card: would our person use this? Not sure: Maybe. Can't agree: leave the card without a choice, or write a wish, and move on. Not every card needs a choice.
  4. When the table chooses We don't need it, the board asks why. Help the table say it in a few words, and save it.
  5. Remind the table about its keeps early: of the cards for the whole board, not per step.
  6. Make sure quieter people get a turn, and that the person clicking isn't the one deciding.

At the end

  1. Help the table give its card dots: the more dots, the more the card matters to its person. Then click Download our board and send the file to BNHR.
  2. After the Data we need tab, download the board again and send the new file: it replaces the first one.
  3. Between the sessions, once BNHR has your file, click Start a new board and choose the afternoon session.

If something goes wrong

  • The internet drops: carry on; the open board keeps working. Only SCeNe and the demo need internet.
  • The laptop fails: use the printed backup set and write the choices down, with the reasons for the cards you don't need; enter them on another laptop afterwards.
  • The board looks empty after reopening: open the laptop's browser that was used before; the board is saved there. If you downloaded it, use Open a saved board.

Online host: share the board on screen in the call, read the group's top wishes aloud at the read-back, and send the board file like the other hosts.
