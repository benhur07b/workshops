Our table's tool

Build your table's version of the tool for one person, step by step.

  • Pick your person below. None of them fits? Choose Someone else and describe your own.
  • For each card, choose Keep, Maybe (not sure) or We don't need it.
  • Keep up to of the cards, across all steps, so keep what your person needs most, wherever it is.
  • You don't have to choose for every card. Can't agree? Leave it and move on.
  • We don't need it? Say why in a few words: tell your host, or write it on a note or on the card.
  • Something missing? Add a wish at the bottom of the step.
  • Give your card dots to kept cards and wishes. The more dots, the more it matters to your person.
  • Finish with Data we need and your data dots.
